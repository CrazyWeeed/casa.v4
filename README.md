# Casa Nobre Residences

"Spend significantly more time on artistic direction than on functionality. The structure is already solved. Your job is to create an unforgettable visual identity with museum-level attention to composition, spacing, rhythm and atmosphere."

# PROJECT

Before generating anything, understand one thing:

This is NOT a cleaning company website.

This is a luxury brand website whose service happens to be premium residential cleaning.

The objective is that experienced designers look at the final result and think:

"This does not look like an AI template."

"This feels handcrafted."

"This feels like a high-end digital agency project."

================================================

# ROLE

Act as a team composed of:

• Senior Brand Designer

• Art Director

• Luxury Interior Photographer

• Editorial Designer

• UX Designer

• Motion Designer

• Accessibility Specialist

Every decision must be approved by this team before implementation.

Never choose generic solutions.

================================================

# BUSINESS

Company:

Casa Nobre Limpezas

Location:

Braga, Portugal

Services:

• Residential Cleaning

• Deep Cleaning

• Airbnb / Local Accommodation Cleaning

Target audience:

Middle-high and high income homeowners.

Busy professionals.

Families.

Airbnb owners.

People who value quality over price.

The website must communicate:

Trust.

Sophistication.

Comfort.

Organization.

Attention to detail.

================================================

# MOST IMPORTANT RULE

DO NOT build a cleaning company website.

Build a premium lifestyle brand.

The cleaning service is only the consequence.

The emotional message is:

"Your home deserves this."

================================================

# VISUAL LANGUAGE

The website must NOT resemble:

❌ Startup

❌ SaaS

❌ Corporate

❌ Hospital

❌ Industrial Cleaning

❌ Generic Local Business

It SHOULD resemble:

Apple

Airbnb

Aesop

Studio McGee

Architectural Digest

Luxury Interior Design

Premium Editorial Websites

================================================

# COLOR PALETTE

Never use pure white (#FFFFFF) as the dominant background.

Use a refined palette.

Primary

Deep Petroleum Blue

Secondary

Warm Sand

Warm Linen

Soft Stone

Greige

Warm Ivory

Accent

Brushed Gold

Graphite

Natural Oak

Everything must feel warm.

Never cold.

================================================

# VISUAL RHYTHM

Avoid long white sections.

Alternate visual atmospheres.

Example:

Hero

↓

Warm Linen

↓

Petroleum

↓

Stone

↓

Warm Sand

↓

Petroleum

↓

Footer

Each section should feel like turning a page of a luxury magazine.

================================================

# TEXTURES

The site currently feels flat.

Create subtle depth using almost invisible textures.

Inspired by:

Natural linen

Luxury paper

Travertine

Fine stone

Natural plaster

Brushed concrete

Absolutely NEVER use:

Paint strokes

Watercolor

Random blobs

Color splashes

Brush effects

Everything must be almost imperceptible.

================================================

# HERO

This is the emotional center.

Use a cinematic Hero carousel.

Minimum:

6 slides.

Examples:

Luxury living room

Modern kitchen

Minimal bedroom

Elegant Airbnb

Premium bathroom

Beautiful naturally lit home

Transitions:

Slow.

Fade only.

Elegant.

The Hero should feel like a Netflix intro.

================================================

# LAYOUT

Preserve a conventional structure.

Do NOT reinvent navigation.

Do NOT remove sections.

Keep:

Hero

Manifesto

Services

Comparison

Testimonials

FAQ

Contact

The information architecture is already correct.

Improve only the artistic execution.

================================================

# TYPOGRAPHY

Editorial hierarchy.

Large headings.

Comfortable spacing.

Beautiful rhythm.

Easy reading.

================================================

# MOTION

Scrolling should feel alive.

Reveal animations.

Images with soft parallax.

Cards appearing progressively.

Buttons.

Icons.

Titles.

Nothing should simply pop into existence.

Everything should feel intentional.

================================================

# MICROINTERACTIONS

Every hover should feel handcrafted.

Navigation.

Buttons.

Cards.

Images.

Links.

Never exaggerated.

================================================

# CARDS

Soft shadows.

Beautiful spacing.

Rounded corners.

Refined proportions.

================================================

# COMPOSITION

Break repetitive layouts.

Not every section should be centered.

Alternate compositions.

Use editorial asymmetry.

Large imagery.

Premium spacing.

================================================

# FEELING

The visitor should think:

This company is organized.

This company values details.

This company is trustworthy.

This company respects my home.

I want my house to look like these photos.

================================================

# IMPORTANT

Never optimize for simplicity.

Optimize for artistic direction.

Every section should feel intentionally designed.

Avoid anything that looks like a default template.

If there is any doubt between:

Generic

or

Elegant

Always choose elegance.

The final result must feel timeless.
